2. 반드시 입에 붙여둘 “만능문장”

문장 전체를 로봇처럼 암기하지 말고, 아래 블록을 자기 경험에 꽂아 넣는다.

A. 시작 10초 — 생각 정리 시간을 자연스럽게 벌기

Hmm, that's an interesting question.생각할 1초 확보. 매번 쓰지는 말기.
Actually, I've thought about this before.의견/선호 질문에 좋음.
If I had to pick one, I'd say ___.선택·선호 질문.
The first thing that comes to mind is ___.경험 하나 꺼낼 때.
There are a few things I could talk about, but the biggest one is ___.AL스럽게 범위를 좁히기.

B. 이유를 “한 단계 더” 설명하기

C. 구체적 경험으로 들어가는 신호

I can't remember the exact name, but ___.고유명사 막힐 때.
Anyway, the point is that ___.길 잃었을 때 본론 회수.

One thing that hasn't changed is ___.변화 질문 확장.
Overall, I'd say it's much more convenient now.마무리 의견.

One reason is ___, and another is ___.2개 이유.
For ordinary people, this means ___.영향 설명.

3. 잘 나오는 질문 “유형”별 90초 답변 구조

① 묘사: Describe your home / neighborhood / favorite place.

IH 한눈에 보이는 핵심 → 2~3개 특징 → 내가 왜 좋아하는지 → 짧은 에피소드

[Direct answer] My favorite place in my neighborhood is a small café near my home. [Picture] It's not very big, but it has large windows and a really relaxed atmosphere. [Personal detail] What I like most is that it's usually quiet, so I can sit there for a long time. [Mini story] I remember going there after a really busy day. I ordered an iced coffee, put my phone away, and just sat by the window for about an hour. [Feeling] I felt much better afterward, so now it's kind of my go-to place when I need a break.

포인트: 가구 5개 나열 X. “이 장소가 내 삶에서 어떤 역할을 하는지”까지.

② 일상/루틴: What do you usually do when you ___?

평소 패턴 → 구체적인 순서 → 예외/선호 → 왜 그렇게 하는지

Usually, when I watch a movie at home, I do it pretty late in the evening. First, I choose something light and entertaining because I don't really want a heavy movie after a long day. Then I get something to drink, turn off the lights, and put my phone on silent. I usually watch movies by myself, but if there's a really fun action movie, I sometimes watch it with someone else. The best part is that I can pause whenever I want, so it's much more comfortable than going to a theater on a weekday.
③ 과거 경험: Tell me about a memorable time when ___.

AL 핵심 배경 → 사건 → 예상 밖 요소 → 내 행동 → 결과 → 회고

[Background] One experience that really stands out happened on a trip I took recently. [Situation] I had planned to visit a few places, but I hadn't organized everything in detail. [Complication] At one point, I realized I was running much later than expected and I still had to get back across the city. [Action] So I quickly changed my plan, checked the transportation options, and decided to skip one stop. [Result] It actually worked out well, and I got back without any major problem. [Reflection] At the time I was stressed, but looking back, it made the trip more memorable and taught me to keep my plans flexible.

AL 느낌을 내는 핵심은 “문제가 있었다 → 대응했다”가 자연스럽게 들어가는 것.

④ 비교: Compare ___ in the past and now.

큰 차이 1개 선언 → 과거 → 현재 → 구체적 사례 → 평가

Compared to the past, the way I watch movies has changed quite a lot. When I was younger, going to the theater felt like the normal way to watch a new movie. These days, I use streaming services much more often because they're convenient and I can choose exactly when to watch something. For example, if I get home late, I can still watch half a movie and finish it the next day. I still enjoy theaters for big action movies, but overall, watching movies at home has become my default.

“과거에는 A, 지금은 B, 또 C…” 식으로 병렬 나열하지 말고 큰 차이 하나를 깊게.

⑤ 선호/이유: What do you like most about ___? Why?

선택 → 이유 → 구체적 장면 → 다른 선택과 짧게 비교 → 결론

If I had to pick one thing, I'd say convenience is what matters most to me. The main reason is that my schedule can get pretty busy, so I don't want something that requires a lot of planning. For example, when I choose a café, I usually prefer a place that's easy to get to and where I can stay comfortably for a while. Of course, a beautiful interior is nice, but if the place is crowded or inconvenient, I probably won't go back. So for me, convenience matters more than appearance.
⑥ 변화/트렌드: How has ___ changed in your country?

AL 변화 선언 → 과거 → 원인 → 현재 사례 → 사람들에게 미친 영향 → 의견

I think the biggest change is how much people rely on mobile apps now. In the past, people had to call or visit a place in person for many simple things. But as smartphones became more common, a lot of services moved online. Now people can make reservations, order food, pay, and even handle customer service from their phones. For ordinary people, this saves a lot of time, although it can also be frustrating when an app doesn't work properly. Overall, though, I think the change has made everyday life much more convenient.
⑦ 문제 경험: Tell me about a time something went wrong.

원래 계획 → 문제 발생 → 왜 문제였는지 → 해결 과정 → 결과 → 배운 점

I remember one time when something went wrong while I was traveling. I was supposed to follow my original schedule, but I realized I didn't have enough time to do everything. The problem was that if I stayed any longer, I might miss the transportation I needed later. So I checked my options, changed the order of my plans, and gave up one activity. Luckily, that solved the problem. It was disappointing for a moment, but it reminded me that when I travel, it's better to prioritize than try to do everything.
⑧ 의견: What do people think about ___? What do you think?

내 입장 → 이유 1 → 실제 영향/예 → 반대쪽 인정 → 최종 판단

Personally, I think using AI tools can be very helpful when they're used for the right kinds of tasks. The biggest benefit is that they can save time on repetitive work. For example, they can help organize information or generate a first draft very quickly. That said, I don't think people should trust every answer automatically, because AI can still make mistakes. So I think the best approach is to use it as a tool, but still check important information yourself.

5. 롤플레이: 이것만큼은 패턴화

5-1. 정보 문의: “전화해서 3~4개 질문하기”

Hi, I'm calling because I'm interested in ___, and I have a few questions. First, could you tell me ___? Also, I'd like to know ___. And one more thing: is it possible to ___? Okay, that sounds good. Thanks for your help. That's all I needed to know.

질문은 어렵게 만들 필요 없다. 가격 / 시간 / 위치 / 예약 / 준비물 / 변경 가능 여부 조합이면 됨.

5-2. 문제 해결: 예약/약속/상품에 문제가 생김

Hi, I'm calling about ___. There's a small problem. I was supposed to ___, but ___. Would it be possible to ___ instead? If that's not available, could I ___? I'm pretty flexible, so another time/day would also work for me. Great, that works for me. Thank you for helping me sort this out.

5-3. 친구에게 상황 설명 + 대안 2개

Hey, I'm really sorry, but something came up and I don't think I can ___ as planned. The problem is ___. How about we ___ instead? Or, if that's difficult, we could ___. Let me know which one works better for you. Sorry again about the change.

5-4. 실제 비슷한 경험 말하기

롤플레이 뒤에 “Have you ever experienced something like this?”가 오면 바로 아래 구조.

Yes, actually, something similar happened to me once. I was trying to ___, but ___. At first, I thought ___. So I ___. Luckily / Unfortunately, ___. Since then, I always ___.

6. 돌발 질문을 모를 때 살아남는 법

주제를 잘 모른다 ≠ 대답 못 한다

예: “recycling system”이 자세히 생각 안 나도 시스템 설명 대신 내가 아는 일상 경험으로 축소.

To be honest, I don't know a lot about the system itself, but I can tell you what I usually do. In my neighborhood, we separate things like plastic, paper, and general waste. At first, I found the rules a little confusing, but I got used to them. Now it's just part of my routine. I think the main reason people do it is to reduce waste and make recycling easier.

문제영어 표현해결
시간 부족I was running out of time.schedule changed / skipped one thing
예약 문제There was a problem with my reservation.called / changed time
길/교통I realized I was going the wrong way.checked map / asked someone

이 여섯 개만 자기 경험에 붙여도 상당수의 “unexpected complication” 질문을 처리할 수 있다.

7. 수요일 저녁 → 일요일 오전 3.5일 플랜

수요일 저녁 — 진단 + 만능 블록

오늘 목표: 영어 공부를 많이 하는 게 아니라 “내가 왜 IM3였는지”를 음성으로 확인.

목요일 — 과거 경험 DAY

에피소드 8개를 만든다. 스크립트 완성문을 외우지 말고 키워드 5개만.

에피소드5개 키워드 예시
최근 여행destination → plan → time problem → changed plan → relieved
카페busy day → quiet café → window → coffee → relaxed
영화friend → action movie → expected little → loved it → recommend
일/학교 프로젝트task → difficulty → tool/method → solved → learned
약속 변경schedule conflict → call → alternative → agreed → lesson
쇼핑/주문 문제wanted item → unavailable → asked → alternative → okay
교통 문제wrong direction → checked map → changed route → arrived
날씨 문제outdoor plan → rain → indoor plan → still fun

훈련: 같은 에피소드를 질문만 바꿔 3번 말하기. “최근 경험 / 기억나는 경험 / 문제 생긴 경험”.

금요일 — 비교 + 변화 + 의견 DAY

금요일부터 한 답변에 과거·현재를 섞어서 말하는 습관을 만든다.

토요일 — 실전 모의 + 롤플레이

금지: 토요일 밤에 AL 템플릿 100문장 새로 외우기 / 발음 완벽주의 / 스크립트 통암기.

일요일 시험 전

  • 자기소개 1회 — 워밍업용. 여기에 체력 쓰지 않는다.
  • 과거 경험 2개, 비교 1개, 롤플레이 1개만 입 풀기.
  • 평소보다 10~15% 천천히. 너무 빨리 말하다 문장이 무너지는 것 방지.
  • 막히면 2초 이상 무음보다 복구문장 사용.
  • “어려운 영어”가 아니라 끊기지 않는 영어.

10. 시험장에서 기억할 7줄

1. 질문에 먼저 답한다.

2. 한 답변에 핵심은 하나만.

3. 이유만 말하지 말고 장면 하나를 보여준다.

4. 경험 질문은 At first → problem/change → so I → in the end.

5. 비교 질문은 biggest difference부터.

6. 모르는 단어는 설명해서 우회.

7. 틀려도 괜찮다. 멈추지 말고 고쳐서 계속 말한다.

기준 메모: ACTFL OPIc는 Functions/Tasks, Accuracy, Context/Content, Text Type를 종합적으로 평가한다. Advanced 범위에서는 주요 시간대의 서술·묘사, 문단 길이의 연결된 발화, 예상치 못한 복잡한 상황 처리 능력이 핵심이다. 이 파일의 질문 목록은 공식 기출 보장이 아니라 해당 기능을 집중 훈련하기 위한 연습 세트다.
